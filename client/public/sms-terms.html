Program

Nalu Technologies Inc provides private AI assistant messaging to the authorized account's saved phone number: responses to your requests, requested images, and optional task-completion updates. This program does not send marketing messages.

Your choice

Follow the signup instructions and text START to +1 (415) 753-4662 to subscribe. Consent is not a condition of purchasing or using other Nalu services. Optional completion updates also require enabling Completion texts in Settings.

Frequency and charges

Message frequency varies with your requests and enabled task updates. Message and data rates may apply under your mobile plan. Contact your mobile provider about its charges.

Unsubscribe and support

Text STOP to unsubscribe; you may receive one confirmation. Text START to resubscribe. Text HELP for help, or contact mberry@n4lu.com. Turning off Completion texts stops those optional updates; text STOP to stop all program messaging.

Delivery and privacy

Delivery depends on carrier approval, network availability, and your device. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Do not use this service for emergencies. Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle mobile information and consent.
