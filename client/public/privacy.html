Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 22, 2026 · Print / Save PDF

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how Nalu — an AI assistant and creative studio available at n4lu.ai and n4lu.com (the "Service"), operated by Hillpointe, LLC and its affiliates ("Nalu", "Company", "we", "us", or "our") — collects, uses, and discloses information about you, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Service.

Plain-language summary: You sign in with an email and password. Your chats, projects, and the documents/designs you create are saved to your account so they sync across your devices. To generate text, images, and video, your prompts are sent to AI model providers that process them on our behalf. We don't sell your personal information. You can delete your content and your account at any time.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information you provide

  • Account information — the email address (or username) and password you use to register, and a display name.
  • Content you create or submit — your prompts and messages, the chats and projects you create, files and attachments you upload, and the documents, spreadsheets, slides, blueprints, logos, images, videos, shopping lists, and other artifacts the Service generates for you ("Content"). This Content is stored and tied to your account so it persists across sessions and devices.
  • Optional credentials — if you connect a GitHub repository, your access token is stored to enable that feature.

B. Information collected automatically

  • Usage and device information — such as IP address, browser type, operating system, and basic interaction data, which may be recorded in server logs for security, troubleshooting, and improving the Service.
  • Local storage — the Service stores a session token and a working copy of your settings and Content in your browser's local storage so the app loads quickly and works offline-first.

We do not run third-party advertising trackers or sell tracking data. We use only the cookies/storage necessary to keep you signed in and to operate the Service.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, secure, and improve the Service and your account.
  • To process your prompts and generate the responses, documents, designs, images, and videos you request.
  • To save and sync your chats, projects, and Content across your devices.
  • To authenticate you, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our terms.
  • To respond to your requests and communicate with you about your account or the Service.

3. AI Model Providers & Service Providers

To generate text, images, and video, the Service sends your prompts and the necessary inputs to third-party AI model and infrastructure providers that process them on our behalf to return a result. We also use hosting, database, and email providers to run the Service. These providers are permitted to use your information only to perform services for us. We do not authorize them to use your Content to send you their own marketing, and we do not sell your personal information.

4. How We Share Information

  • With service providers as described in Section 3.
  • When you choose to share — for example, if you publish a generated website via a share link, the content of that artifact becomes accessible to anyone who has the link.
  • For legal reasons — to comply with the law or a valid legal request, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of Nalu, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

5. Data Retention & Your Choices

  • Access & deletion — you can delete individual chats and projects in the app at any time. To delete your account and associated Content, contact us at the address below.
  • Sign-out — signing out clears the working copy of your data from that browser; your account Content remains saved server-side until you delete it.
  • We retain information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, and as required by law.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted transport (HTTPS) and hashed passwords. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in a manner inconsistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If we learn we have done so, we will delete it.

8. International Users

Nalu is operated in the United States and the information we collect is governed by U.S. law. If you access the Service from outside the U.S., your information may be transferred to and processed in the U.S., which may have different data-protection laws than your country.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted; your continued use of the Service indicates your acceptance of the updated Policy.
