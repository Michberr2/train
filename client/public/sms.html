Nalu assistant messaging

Receive answers from your Nalu assistant, images you request, and optional task-completion updates by SMS and MMS.

This private program is for the authorized Nalu account using its saved phone number. It is not a public messaging list. Delivery is pending carrier campaign approval.

Subscribe from your saved number

After carrier activation, text START to +1 (415) 753-4662.

By sending START, you agree to receive automated assistant replies, requested images, and optional task updates from Nalu Technologies Inc at the number you use to subscribe. Message frequency varies with your requests and settings. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Text STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help.

Read our SMS terms and Privacy Policy before subscribing. Your mobile information and consent will not be sold or shared for marketing or promotional purposes.

Use your assistant

Once subscribed and activated, text your question or image request to the same number. Enable Completion texts in Nalu Settings if you also want updates when tasks finish. Saving a phone number alone does not subscribe you.

Stop or get help

Text STOP to end messaging. Text START to resubscribe. Text HELP or email mberry@n4lu.com for assistance.
