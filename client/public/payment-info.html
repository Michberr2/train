Payment information and refunds

Information about optional, one-time tips for Nalu assistance you have already received.

A tip is optional and helps cover Nalu’s operating costs. It adds no AI usage credits, extra features, or guaranteed future access.

What your payment is for

You can leave a tip for the Nalu AI service you have already used. This is a tip for a software service, not a charitable donation. Payment goes to Nalu’s operator; it does not create a wallet owned by an AI.

Each payment is a single tip. It does not start a subscription, recurring payment, or automatic top-up. Your payment history records past tips and refunds, not a spendable account balance.

Amounts and checkout

The available amounts are $10, $25, and $50 in United States dollars (USD). Review the amount shown in Stripe Checkout before paying.

Stripe hosts the payment page and processes your payment details. Nalu does not receive your full card number. You can leave checkout before completing payment.

After checkout, Nalu confirms the payment with Stripe before showing it as paid. Some payments may take time to confirm. Check your payment history before making another payment if the status is still processing.

Refund requests

To ask about a payment or request a refund, email mberry@n4lu.com. Include your Nalu account email, the payment date and amount, and the receipt or payment reference if available. Do not send your password or full card details.

Refund requests are reviewed case by case. We may ask for information needed to identify the payment and understand your request. If a refund is approved, it is submitted through Stripe to the original payment method; your bank or payment provider determines when it appears.

Payment records and privacy

Nalu keeps payment references, amounts, status, and refund history associated with your account so you can review payments and receive support. Stripe processes payment information to complete transactions. See the Nalu privacy policy and Stripe privacy policy for more information.
